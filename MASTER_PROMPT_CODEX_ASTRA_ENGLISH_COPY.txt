Switching camera or visual mode must not remount the simulation engine, start a parallel race, advance a sector, or reset a save. Preserve 0.50× and 0.75× playback, AUTO, speeds through 16×, pause, +SECTOR, Race Control, and all five driver commands.

### SPRINT 02 — Babylon.js TV broadcast director (P0; greatest visual impact)

**Primary files:** `src/presentation/race-camera.ts`, `babylon-camera.ts`, `src/features/live-map.tsx`, `live-race-babylon.tsx`. Create `src/presentation/broadcast-director.ts` only if extracting it reduces coupling. **Do not duplicate or rewrite `pickShot()` unnecessarily.** Reuse its existing AUTO decisions, battles, race events, starts, and priorities, adapting the selected `Shot` into the 3D camera system.

Support TV broadcast, duel, close isometric, driver-follow, strategic/aerial, pit-lane, and legacy onboard cameras. A manually selected camera must remain active until the user returns to AUTO. The system should anticipate corners and frame groups of 2–4 cars. Initial **experimental settings, not measured existing values**: TV camera height y=3.5–7 units, behind=8–15 units, lateral offset=3–7 units, FOV 42–58°; follow camera y=2.8–5.5 units, behind=5–10 units, FOV 50–62°; a higher strategic view fitted to the group; pit camera moving through entry → stop → exit anchors. Adapt all values to the project's actual coordinate scale.

**Fix frame-rate-dependent camera smoothing.** Replace fixed `Lerp(..., .09/.28)` factors with `alpha = 1 - exp(-dt / tau)` for both camera position and look-at target. Remove target snapping caused by `setTarget`; interpolate target as well. Proposed `tau`: 0.22–0.45 seconds, to be tuned. Use deliberate cuts with brief fades when angle changes are too large, rather than passing through barriers or buildings. If a camera is obstructed, evaluate an alternate anchor; preserve the existing `sceneryVisibility` fallback. Cameras must not influence race positions.

**Tests:** 32-car race start; leader coverage; P1/P2 duel; P10/P11 duel; rain; Safety Car; manual camera versus AUTO; reduced-motion mode; device rotation; 30/60 FPS; record real 15–30-second clips. Do not report “TV camera complete” if the camera merely follows one car without meaningfully showing battles in real race scenarios.

### SPRINT 04 — Reuse GLB models and add procedural animation (P1)

The baseline includes 16 teams and 32 GLBs with nodes resembling `FrontLeftWheel_`, `FrontRightWheel_`, `Rear*`, body meshes, and materials, **but no embedded animation clips**. Reuse these assets; do not rebuild every car in Blender unless inspection demonstrates a genuine need. Create appropriate wheel parent pivots; animate wheel rotation according to `omega = visualSpeed / visualWheelRadius`, deriving movement from interpolated display travel and keeping it decoupled from official results. Pause and reduced-motion accessibility modes must stop nonessential micro-animations; 0.50× should scale corresponding animation tempo. When switching `high`/`mobile` quality, safely clear instances/caches or defer asset switching to a safe scene transition without losing race state.

### SPRINT 05 — Lightweight 3D scenery, prioritized around the camera (P1)

### SPRINT 06 — Cinematic pit stops grounded in simulation (P1)

Reuse `callPit()` and the real `now`, `next`, and `cancel` commands, together with the existing `pitPhase`/`pitStage` state and pit events. The HUD must clearly display tire-compound selection, scheduled status, confirmation, and permitted cancellation. Test compound changes, genuine sporting consequences, two pit calls for the same team, and cancellation. The post-stop order must come from the authoritative engine, never from the animation.

### SPRINT 07 — Unified Babylon quality control and fallback (P1)

The current implementation applies two different `setHardwareScalingLevel` formulas when quality changes. Consolidate them into one DPR/profile controller, updated exactly once per change unless an explicitly controlled exception is required. Provide a working **offline 2D fallback** when WebGL is unavailable or its context is lost; avoid mandatory remote `importBabylon()` if the bundled local engine is available. Review PWA/service-worker caching to avoid mismatched bundles and GLB assets after deployment. Test cold/warm starts and restoring from background.
